Delivery Information

Delivery Areas

We deliver nationwide across South Africa, to both major metros and outlying areas.

Delivery Times

Orders are typically processed within 1-2 business days and delivered within 2-5 business days thereafter, depending on your location.

Delivery Fees

Delivery fees are calculated at checkout based on your location and order size. Free delivery may apply on qualifying orders — this will be shown at checkout if applicable.

Order Tracking

Once your order has shipped, you'll receive tracking details so you can follow its progress to your door.

Delivery Issues

If your order hasn't arrived within the expected timeframe, please contact us and our team will help resolve it.
